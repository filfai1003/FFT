Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

The short version

  • FFT keeps your workouts, food, body data and photos on your device. With an account you can choose to also keep a copy on our server (in the EU), so you can use other devices, the web app and AI apps. It is optional: you choose when you create the account, and turning it off deletes the copy on the server.
  • Weight, food, workouts and photos are health data. We use them only to run the app for you, and only with your consent.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • Some data goes to others only when you use a feature that needs it: the AI coach, food search, AI apps you connect.
  • The community leaderboards are optional: if you join, other members see your name, level, badges and scores (never your weight, measurements, progress photos or logs), and the profile photo and look you choose to add. Leaving deletes all of it (section 3).
  • You can export everything, and delete your account and data, at any time in the app: Profile > Settings > Your data.

The data controller for FFT is Filippo Faiella, a private individual. You can write to us about anything in this policy at filfai1003@gmail.com.

This policy covers the FFT app (Android and the web app), its server, the personal API and MCP server, and this website.

2. Two ways to use FFT

  • With an account (email and password). Your data is saved on each device you use. When you create the account the app asks whether to keep a copy on our server; nothing is preselected and nothing is sent before you choose.
    • Copy on the server on: your data is synced in the background with our server, so it is the same on every device and in the web app, AI apps you connect (Claude, ChatGPT, your own tools) can use it, and you get it back if you lose your phone. The app works offline and sends changes when it can. You choose what goes into the copy, by category: body (weight, body fat, measurements, body goals), diet (food log, your own foods, meal plans, calorie targets, water, supplements), training (workout plans, workouts and sets, your own exercises and their pictures) and progress photos. All are on unless you turn some off. Your profile (name, birth date, sex, height, goal), settings and consents are always part of the copy, because your other devices need them to start. A category you turn off is deleted from our server at once and is never sent again (the server also refuses it); AI apps and the API cannot see it.
    • End-to-end encryption (optional, for the whole copy): your data and photos are encrypted on your phone, with a key only you have, before they are sent. The key is protected by a passphrase you choose (not your login password) and by a recovery key shown to you once; we keep the key only in that locked form and never see your passphrase or recovery key. Our server then stores data it cannot read: we can only see your account, how many items there are, their size, when they changed and which kind of record each one is (for example "water log"). The API and AI apps cannot work while encryption is on. If you lose both the passphrase and the recovery key, nobody (us included) can read the copy on the server; your phone keeps its data. Turning encryption off sends your data again in readable form and deletes the encrypted copy.
    • Copy on the server off: your data stays on the phone. The server only keeps your account (email, password and an empty profile and settings record). The web app, other devices and AI apps cannot see your data.
    • Sync with another phone (optional, only with the copy on the server off): your phones exchange changes through our server, end-to-end encrypted with a key that only your phones have (it goes from phone to phone in a QR code and never reaches the server). The server keeps no copy: each message is deleted as soon as the phone it is for has received it, and any message not delivered within 30 days is deleted anyway. It sees only your account, a random id and the model name of each paired phone, and the size and time of each message. You choose the categories that are exchanged, as for the copy on the server.
    You can change this at any time in Settings > Your data > Keep a copy on the server. Turning it off deletes the copy of your data and photos on our server right away (it cannot be undone; the phone keeps everything). Turning it on again sends everything on the phone. Accounts created before this choice existed keep their copy until they turn it off.
  • Without an account ("Use without an account"). Everything stays on that phone only. We receive nothing about you, except anonymous error reports (section 4) and the food search words if you search online (section 6). Nothing is backed up: if you lose the phone, the data is gone.

4. Legal bases

  • Contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)): your account and running the app and its features for you.
  • Explicit consent (art. 9(2)(a)) for health data: weight, body data, food, workouts and photos. It is required to use the app, because a fitness and diet tracker cannot work without storing them: if you do not agree, no profile is set up and nothing is stored. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings > Your data; the app then pauses until you give it again, export your data or delete your account, and our server stops using your data too: the API, MCP, connected AI apps and the included AI coach are refused for your account (and when the server cannot check your consent, the included AI coach refuses rather than sending anything). Withdrawing does not affect what was done before.
  • A separate explicit consent (art. 9(2)(a) and 6(1)(a)) for the AI coach: the first time you open it, the app explains what is sent and to whom and asks you to agree (nothing is preselected and nothing is sent before you agree). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings > Your data; the coach then asks again before it is used.
  • Consent (art. 6(1)(a)) for the community: joining is your choice and leaving withdraws it (section 3). Only scores and badges are shared, not health data itself.
  • Legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)): keeping the service secure (rate limits, sign-in records, admin change log) and fixing bugs (error reports). You can object (section 9).
  • Legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c)) when the law requires us to keep or hand over data.

5. Where your data is kept

  • On your device, always: in the app's own storage on the phone, or in the browser for the web app. On the phone your data lives in the app's private storage, protected by the phone's own encryption (Android encrypts app storage by default) and screen lock; the app does not add an encryption layer of its own, except for your sign-in and your own AI key, which it stores encrypted with the phone's secure storage. You can also blur progress photos and lock them behind Face ID, fingerprint or passcode, lock the whole app (asked when it opens and after a few minutes away), and keep it out of screenshots and the recent-apps preview (on by default for the progress photo screens): Settings > Phone privacy.
  • On our server, for accounts that keep a copy there (optional, section 2): hosted by Railway, in the European Union (Amsterdam, Netherlands). Only Railway's private network reaches the database; every table has rules so that each account can only read and change its own rows. Progress photos are in a private storage area, reachable only through short-lived signed links. They and the rest of the copy are end-to-end encrypted only if you turn that on (section 2); otherwise our server can read them.
  • Backups: every night the database and the photos are copied, encrypted (AES-256-GCM, with a key kept apart from the copies), into a storage bucket of our hosting in the same EU region. They are kept for at most 6 months: daily copies for 30 days, then one copy a month for 6 months. If we ever restore one, the accounts deleted after it was made are deleted again (we keep a list of deleted account ids, nothing else, for that), and so are the entries and photos deleted for good after it was made (from the markers described in section 7). For accounts with end-to-end encryption on, the backups hold only the encrypted copy, photos included: we cannot read it there either.

Our team can technically access the server database to run and repair the service. We look at the content of your data only when needed to fix a problem you reported, at your request, or when the law requires it. The admin panel hides the content of your data and every password hash by default; showing them needs an extra unlock and is logged, and so is every page of data our team opens and every database query it runs. The panel cannot download copies of the database: the only copies are the encrypted nightly backups.

  • Open Food Facts (Open Food Facts association, France): when you search for a food or scan a barcode, only the search words or the barcode are sent (through our server, or directly from the phone without an account). Nothing about you.

  • Google Play (Google): if you install FFT from Google Play, Google processes your download and device data under its own terms.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We may disclose data if the law or an authority requires it.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and app data: the copy on the server is kept until you turn it off or delete your account (then it is deleted at once; it may remain in the backups until they expire). Messages between your paired phones (section 2) are kept until the phone they are for receives them, at most 30 days. What you delete in the app (an entry, a workout, a photo, including a photo replaced by a new one) is first marked as deleted, so it can be undone and your other devices learn about it, and is deleted for good, with its photo files, after 7 days: on the server and on your devices. Only a marker (table and row id, no content) stays for 190 days, so devices that were offline delete it too and a restored backup deletes it again. An own exercise still used in a past workout stays (hidden) until that workout is deleted. Deleted data may remain in the backups until they expire. All of it goes when you delete your account.
  • Community (section 3): your member entry, friendships and profile look (photo included) until you remove them, leave the community or delete your account (a removed photo's file is deleted within a day); each week's and month's scores at most 70 days after that period ends; friend requests nobody answered, 30 days.
  • Consent history: until you delete your account.
  • AI usage records: until you delete your account.
  • Error reports: 60 days, then deleted automatically. The ones sent while you were signed in are deleted with your account; the ones sent without an account carry no account id and expire after 60 days. A report sent after the deletion by a phone that was still signed in is stored without the account id.
  • Password reset requests: at most 30 days after you sent them (handled or not), and deleted with your account. A temporary password is sent only to the email address of the account.
  • Admin change log and sign-in records: at most 12 months.
  • Our hosting provider's technical logs (Railway): error messages of our services and, for some requests, the IP address, browser and the address requested. We keep sign-in details (email, IP address) out of them where the software allows. They are kept for a limited time set by Railway's service plan, cannot be deleted per account, and expire by themselves.
  • Data on your devices: until you delete the account in the app or remove the app (or clear the browser's site data for the web app). Signing out keeps the data on that device, so it is there when you sign in again.

8. Deleting your account

In the app: Profile > Settings > Your data > Delete account. This deletes, right away, your account on the server with all its data and photos, and all the data on that device, including your AI conversations there. Your data may remain in the server backups until they expire (at most 6 months: daily copies for 30 days, then one copy a month for 6 months), then it is gone. On other devices where you used FFT, remove the app (or clear the browser's site data) to remove the copy there. On a phone you can instead choose Delete the account, keep my data on this phone: the account and its server copy are deleted the same way, but the data on that phone (photos and AI conversations included) moves to FFT without an account, where it stays only on that phone, with no online backup; Premium and the AI coach end with the account. You can also ask us by email. Details: how account deletion works.

10. Security

All connections to our server are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Our hosting provider, Railway, is SOC 2 Type II certified, and its data processing agreement with us commits it to keep the database encrypted at rest. Our nightly backups are, in addition, encrypted by us (AES-256-GCM) before they are stored. With end-to-end encryption on (section 2), your copy on the server is encrypted on your phone with XChaCha20-Poly1305 under a random 256-bit key, itself locked with your passphrase (scrypt) and your recovery key; the server never receives the key, the passphrase or the recovery key. The database has no public address and each account can only reach its own rows. Passwords and API tokens are stored only as hashes. Requests are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure: if a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authority as the law requires.
