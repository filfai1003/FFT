4. Not medical advice

FFT, its targets, suggestions, exercise instructions and the AI coach are for general fitness and information only. They are not medical advice and do not replace a doctor, dietitian or qualified trainer. Ask a professional before starting a new diet or training plan, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, an injury, or a history of eating disorders. Stop and seek help if you feel pain or unwell while training.

For safety, the app does not let you set a calorie target below 1,500 kcal per day or more than 25% below your estimated maintenance calories. For targets outside these limits, follow a plan from a nutrition professional.
